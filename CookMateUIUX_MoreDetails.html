CookMate UI/UX Design

Nov 2026 - Nov 2026

Designed the full UI/UX experience for CookMate, an AI-powered smart cooking companion mobile application aimed at improving the home cooking experience. The project focused on creating a clean, modern, and user-friendly interface with smooth navigation from onboarding to recipe discovery and step-by-step cooking assistance.
Created wireframes, high-fidelity UI screens, and consistent design systems while following user-centered design principles. The design included features such as personalized recipe recommendations, dietary preference selection, favourites management, and interactive cooking guidance. The project also involved mascot concept design and AI-assisted visual generation for branding and application engagement.
